  • Maybe skip for nowCoffee, alcohol and fruit juice can irritate your bladder. And sadly, cranberry won't treat an infection.

Please call your doctor today if you get

  • Fever, chills or shivering
  • Pain in your back, just under the ribs
  • Nausea or vomiting
  • Blood in your pee (pink, red or dark brown)
  • Symptoms getting worse quickly, or no better after 2 days on the meds
  • A new rash after taking your meds

Go to the emergency room right away, and don't drive yourself, if you feel confused or very drowsy, have trouble speaking or breathing, or your face or throat swells.
